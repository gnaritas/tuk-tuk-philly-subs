The story behind the name

A tuk-tuk is Bangkok's three-wheeler. This shop is its Richmond cousin.

“Tuk-tuks are connected with Thailand and very much where they originated, and so we wanted to pay homage to our roots.” — Jon Niemiec, co-owner

Joy Supanya and Jon Niemiec — who own The British Embassy three doors down — partnered with Fredericksburg restaurateur Marco Metry, who came up working Philly cheesesteak shops in South Philadelphia.

The name nods to Joy's Thai heritage; the menu nods to Marco's Philly schooling. The spot itself — the former Sub Central storefront on East Main Street — was picked for one crowd: downtown workers looking for a serious lunch. Counter service, tables to eat in, everything built to order.
