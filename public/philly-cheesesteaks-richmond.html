How the steaks come

Chopped steak hits the hot side of the flat-top; onions go down beside it so they pick up the grill, not just the steam.

Cheese goes on at the moment it melts through instead of smothering the steak — cheddar cheese sauce on the Tuk Tuk Classic, Provolone on the Italiano.

The roll gets toasted just long enough to hold sauce without turning into cracker. Patience, then speed.

Order it any way you like it — the register speaks fluent wiz-adjacent. And every build works as a gluten-free wrap.

Why trust the steak

Philly homework, Richmond griddle

Metry spent his cheesesteak education in Philadelphia shops before partnering with Joy Supanya and Jon Niemiec — the owners of The British Embassy three doors down — on this shop, which opened in October 2026 in the former Sub Central space.

The result is a steak sub counter that takes its timing seriously and its portions generously: eight- and twelve-inch subs, gyros, burgers and salads on the same board.
