Built for noon

Lunch is the mission

Co-owner Jon Niemiec was blunt about who this shop is for: the downtown office crowd, with lunch as the busiest stretch of the day. The old Sub Central dining room — counter, tables, quick turns — is set up exactly that way.

Order at the counter and eat in, grab it to go, or have it delivered on Uber Eats. Monday through Saturday, lunch and dinner, while the exact hours get finalized.
