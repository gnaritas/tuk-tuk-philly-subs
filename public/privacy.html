Plain-English privacy

What this site collects

The short version: nothing tracks you, nothing is sold, and the only information this site ever gathers is what you type into the contact form.

The contact form

When you send a message, your name, email, optional phone number and message are delivered to the shop's owners so they can reply. They're kept for that purpose — answering you — and nothing else. We don't run the form through an advertising network.

Cookies and trackers

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies of its own and loads no tracking pixels or third-party scripts. If you open the Google map on the home or contact pages, Google may apply its own policies to that request; you can get directions from the links instead if you prefer.

Ordering links

Delivery runs through Uber Eats. When you click through to order, you're on their site under their terms and privacy policy — this site doesn't receive your order data back.

Your choices

Want your message deleted after we've answered it? Email info@britembassy.com from the address you wrote with and say so. Questions about this page reach the same address.
